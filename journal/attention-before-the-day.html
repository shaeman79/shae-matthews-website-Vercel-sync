Body & energy · Editorial draft

Before the day borrows your attention.

A small invitation to choose how you enter the morning.

Before opening the first stream of messages, what do you want this morning to contain? This is an invitation to reflect, not a claim that one routine will solve every difficulty.

Make the beginning small

Choose an action that fits the morning you actually have. Open the curtains, sit quietly for a moment, drink something you enjoy, or write down the one task that matters today.

The action can be ordinary. Its purpose is to create a moment in which you choose, rather than borrow a ready-made picture of how a disciplined man should live.

Protect the useful part

After a few mornings, ask what helped and what merely added effort. Keep what serves you. Adapt the routine when work, family, health or circumstances require it.

There is no score to win here. The question is whether the beginning of the day feels more deliberately yours.
